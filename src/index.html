Getting started

Ten short steps. Voicy will take you to each thing and wait while you use it. There is a practice round in the middle - nobody is listening and nothing is scored.

    Practice round

    Just you. No timer, no vote, no score, and nothing is kept afterwards. Say whatever comes to mind.

    Privacy and anonymity

    Submissions are anonymous to the room. These are the switches that keep it that way, and the one place a caption can still leak.

    Transcript only Audio never leaves the device
    Share transcript, not audio Other players see words, never your voice.
    New anonymous handle each round Stops a player linking round one to round five.
    Voiceprint guard Flags captions that sound like one you already submitted.
    Wipe local buffer on exit Clears drafts, drafts of captions and the mic test.
    Where a caption can still leak

    A cloud transcription engine receives your audio, so it - not you - is the only party that never hears it. An on-device engine keeps that promise. Voiceprints are the other hole: two captions in the same room from the same throat can be matched by ear. The guard above checks for that, but it is a warning, not a wall.
